Controller mappings

Community-made MIDI mappings. The easiest way to install one is inside the app — Settings → MIDI → Get mappings — but every mapping here can also be downloaded as a file.

Install a downloaded mapping

01

In Talea, open Settings → MIDI.

02

Click Import mapping… and pick the downloaded .json file (or drop it into the mappings folder shown there).

03

Connect your controller and select the mapping — done.

Share your own

01

Map your controller in Talea (MIDI learn does most of the work).

02

Open a pull request adding your mapping file to the talea-mappings repository.

03

Once merged it appears here and inside every copy of the app — automatically.
